# Trademark policy

"Tandem" and the Tandem logos identify this project. The AGPL license covers the code, not the name.

**You may, without asking:**
- Say your product works with, is based on, or is a fork of Tandem, and link to the project.
- Use the name Tandem to refer to the unmodified software as distributed by the project.
- Run the unmodified software and call it Tandem.

**You may not, without written permission:**
- Use "Tandem" or a confusingly similar name, or the logos, as the name of a modified version, a hosted service
  or a product built on it.
- Suggest the project's authors endorse, sponsor or support your product or service.
- Remove the "Based on Tandem" credit that the additional terms (`LICENSE-TERMS.md`) require.

If you are unsure, ask the maintainers first. Rebranding a fork under a different name is fine, as long as the
attribution stays.
